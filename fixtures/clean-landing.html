What Remit does

  • Invoices from your price list

    Pick the customer and the items. Tax rates, payment terms and the purchase order number fill in from the customer record.

  • Reminders on your schedule

    A short email three days before the due date, another on the day, then every seven days until it is paid. You write the wording once and can switch reminders off for any customer.

  • Payments matched to invoices

    Connect your bank feed and Remit marks invoices paid when the money arrives, including part payments. Anything it cannot match waits for you to confirm.

  • One list of who owes what

    Unpaid invoices sorted by how late they are, with the contact name and phone number beside each amount.

Setting up takes an afternoon

  1. Import your customers

    Upload a CSV or connect your accounting software. Names, addresses, tax numbers and payment terms come across.

  2. Set your reminder schedule

    Choose when reminders go out and what they say. Customers with their own terms can have their own schedule.

  3. Connect your bank feed

    Remit reads incoming payments and matches them by amount and reference. You review the ones it is unsure of.
